Privacy Policy for Alif Kids

Last updated: October 29, 2026
Commitment to Children's Privacy: Alif Kids is designed specifically for young learners and families. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Alif Kids is an educational offline-first mobile application. We do not collect, request, or store any personal information from children or parents, including:

  • No real names, home addresses, or phone numbers
  • No email addresses or social media logins
  • No audio recordings or microphone data
  • No precise physical GPS location
  • No photos, camera footage, or user files

2. Device-Stored Local Progress

All learning progress (such as completed Arabic letters, traced numbers 1-100, earned stickers, and optional child nickname) is stored strictly on your local device storage using standard local Android preferences. This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any external server.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Alif Kids integrates Google AdMob to support ongoing development while providing free access to children worldwide. To protect our young learners, our advertising setup is strictly configured in full compliance with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA:

  • Child-Directed Treatment: Configured with TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT_TRUE and TAG_FOR_UNDER_AGE_OF_CONSENT_TRUE.
  • Content Rating G: Ads are strictly filtered to general audience content (G-rating only), ensuring age-appropriate educational and family-friendly advertisements.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: Personalized, interest-based tracking and profiling of children is completely disabled.
  • Mindful Intervals: Advertisements only appear after completing a full letter learning journey, backed by a strict 3-minute cooldown period so learning is never spammed or interrupted.

4. Smart Notifications

Alif Kids includes optional local learning reminders. These notifications are scheduled locally on the device via the Android AlarmManager and do not require internet access or remote notification servers.

5. Parental Control & Support

Parents may clear all local progress, manage notifications, or remove advertisements via device settings at any time.
